The Immune system

The outer defense: Skin - a waterproof organ prevents viruses and bacteria from sneaking in. The stomach vomits the germs out or blasted out by a sneeze or cough. Sometimes germs can beat the skin and sneak in. Germs make you feel unwell by it's released toxins. The fighting of this can cause, fever/aching joints/inflammation. Inner defense: Complement proteins crows towards the germs and send out a sensation of soreness or inflammation. They alarm the phagocytes (macrophages, granulocytes, dendritic cells) which eat the invading germs. If the germs multiply enough, they can beat the first inner defense. Second inner defense shows white blood cells called lymphocytes. T-Helpers search for any antibodies on phagocytes. Killer T Cells, remove cells infected by viruses.
